Either a site path ("/assets/img/sponsors/example-corp.svg") or a full https:// URL. 2. A file dropped in assets/img/sponsors/ named after them: "Example Corp" -> assets/img/sponsors/example-corp.svg. Matched on the slugified basename, so the extension and the capitalisation don't matter.
