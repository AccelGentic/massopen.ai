About Mass Open

The intersection of open source and AI in New England

Mass Open exists to build a center of gravity in New England for open source AI. To do that, we are creating 2 types of events to serve the AI community in New England and the Acela Corridor.

Agentic 101

Agentic 101 is a series of workshops where attendees will learn how to deploy and manage local agentic systems. Agentic engineering holds the promise of automating complex, multi-step operations once reserved for brittle scripts and manual governance and observation. In Agentic 101, you’ll learn the basics for buiding and running these systems, the open source projects powering them, and ways to make use of them in your daily work without accruing large token usage fees.

See our events section to find out when we're coming to your neck of the woods.

Mass Open

The Mass Open Developer Conference (MODC) will be our signature event, bringing together open source developers, startup founders, investors, enterprise decision makers, and practitioners. MODC aims to expand the open source AI community and move it forward, particularly in Boston and all of New England. Topics will include AI safety and security, managing agentic swarms, reducing dependencies on frontier models, and building on the concept of local agentic AI deployments that maximize sovereignty and privacy. MODC will be coming to Boston in April of 2027. We'll share more details here as they're available.
