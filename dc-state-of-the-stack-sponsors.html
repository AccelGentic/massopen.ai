Additional Sponsorships

Add-ons and standalones.

Can be purchased separately from Platinum, Gold, or Silver sponsorships — either in addition to those or as stand-alone investments.

Hands-on Workshop (2)

$2,000

Platinum sponsors only: dedicated room and 90-minute time slot for up to 30 participants during afternoon open spaces. We will assist with pre-event workshop registration.

Social Hour

Negotiable

The American Red Cross is an alcohol-free facility. If you'd like to host an informal gathering after the event at a nearby venue, we'll help you find the right space and get the word out. Social Hour sponsors get signage, social media recognition, and the chance to bring the community together off-site.

Lanyard Sponsor (1)

$4,500

Your logo on every attendee's lanyard for two days — worn by engineers, operators, and leaders from across the DC tech community. Single color logo. Final placement, size, and color subject to organizer approval.

Closed Captioning Sponsor (1)

$4,500

We believe every attendee deserves to follow the conversation. Your logo appears on screen as talks are transcribed live — visible to everyone in the room, both days.
