About Mass Open

Mass Open is an informal community initiative focused on open-source and open-weight AI in Massachusetts, New England, and the Acela corridor. We bring people together to learn how to build and use AI systems, share what they learn, and explore alternatives to dependence on a single vendor.

Open-source and open-weight models are central to that work. We also care about access to knowledge and a regional community where people can learn from one another. Model licenses differ: access to a model's weights alone does not make it open source.

Events and learning

We are planning the Mass Open Developer Conference (MODC) in Boston as our flagship gathering for the open AI community.

Our Agentic 101 workshops focus on building and running AI systems that use tools and carry out tasks across multiple steps. Sessions cover the software behind these systems and practical questions about safety, security, and control.

We are interested in exploring other event formats where community members can share their work and learn together. See our events for current programs.
